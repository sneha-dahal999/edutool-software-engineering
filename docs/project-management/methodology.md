# Development Method and Project Management

The project uses Agile development with Scrum practices. Work is organised around a prioritised backlog and short iterations.

## Team allocation
- Dinuwan Kavinda Karunathilaka: requirements and project analysis
- Sneha Dahal: architecture, detailed design and prototype
- Adarsha Panta: repository evidence, QA, testing, timeline and budget

## Change control
A proposed requirement change should record its reason and effect on scope, time, cost, design and testing. The team then accepts, delays or rejects it.
